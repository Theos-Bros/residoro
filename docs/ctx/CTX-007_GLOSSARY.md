# Purpose

Defines standard terminology used throughout Residoro documentation and implementation.

---

# Brokerage

A real estate company operating within Residoro.

---

# Workspace

The isolated operational environment belonging to a single brokerage.

---

# User

A person with authenticated access to a workspace.

---

# Contact

A person or organization with whom the brokerage has a relationship.

---

# Company

A legal entity associated with contacts, developers, vendors, or clients.

---

# Buyer

A contact seeking to purchase or lease property.

---

# Seller

A contact marketing property through the brokerage.

---

# Owner

The legal owner of a property.

---

# Property

A physical real estate asset.

---

# Listing

A brokerage's marketing representation of a property.

---

# Deal

A transaction between parties managed by the brokerage.

---

# Pipeline

A sequence of business stages representing progress toward completion.

---

# Stage

The current business state within a pipeline.

---

# Activity

An action that has already occurred.

---

# Checklist

A set of operational tasks required within a stage.

---

# Capability

A business function provided by Residoro independent of implementation.

---

# Business Domain

A major operational area of the Brokerage Operating System.

---

# Business Entity

A core object representing a business concept such as Contact, Property, Listing, or Deal.

---

# Row Level Security (RLS)

Database policies that restrict data access based on the authenticated user's workspace and permissions.

---

# Shared-Schema Multi-Tenant Architecture

An architecture where multiple brokerages share one database schema while remaining logically isolated through tenant-aware design and security policies.

---

# AI-Assisted Migration

The process of using AI to map, validate, preview, and prepare data for import into Residoro before user approval.
